## About the Project

OSRA is a forthcoming solution for risk management across all Alphabet enterprises worldwide. The client required a proof of concept within an extremely tight one-month deadline to validate the approach before committing to full-scale development.

## The Challenge

Develop a proof of concept within tight deadlines with a complete absence of initial requirements. The deadline was one month from kickoff to stakeholder presentation. A particular challenge was that at the start of design, all functionality was handled through primitive Google Forms questionnaires.

## My Role

A player-coach. As the Lead Designer, I oversaw the end-to-end design process. Within tight timelines, I organized the team, established effective cross-functional collaboration under extreme time pressure, ensured continuous feedback collection, and presented design solutions to stakeholders.

## Process

### Pre-Discovery & Guidelines Analysis

Conducted rapid pre-discovery phase analyzing existing risk management solutions. We carefully studied the POC requirements and compiled a prioritized list. We also took into account Google's Material Design guidelines and internal UI standards. This accelerated the design process by establishing a solid foundation from day one.

![Requirements analysis and feature mapping](/images/cases/osra/requirements.png)

### User Meetings & Requirements Clarification

Organized intensive user meetings with Google representatives to rapidly clarify requirements that were initially undefined. Conducted focused interviews to understand risk management workflows, user roles, and critical pain points across Alphabet's diverse enterprise landscape.

To deliver the project within tight deadlines, I designed a series of rapid prototypes that went through multiple rounds of testing with Google representatives on real-world risk management scenarios. ### Mobile Version for Asset Management

After studying how managers work on-site, I initiated the development of a mobile version of the application. The primary use case was asset inventory, risk logging, and control tracking directly in the field. This enabled assessors to manage risk management items on the go while maintaining full data context on their mobile devices.

## Results

Transformed a complex UX tool request into a streamlined scenario logic that was easy to understand and navigate. Proved that both desktop and mobile solutions were viable within the proposed architecture. The prototype aligned with user expectations and was approved by stakeholders, laying the foundation for the next product development phase.

## Key Learnings

Gained invaluable experience working with a large vendor under extremely tight time constraints. Learned the importance of identifying potential issues early in the process and establishing clear communication channels from day one.

The case confirmed: high delivery speed is compatible with a working UX solution when processes are well-organized.
