## About the Project

OSRA is a forthcoming solution for risk management across all Alphabet enterprises worldwide. The client required a proof of concept within an extremely tight one-month deadline to validate the approach before committing to full-scale development.

## The Challenge

Create a proof of concept in the shortest possible time with no initially described requirements. The deadline was one month from project kickoff to stakeholder presentation.

## My Role

Lead Designer overseeing all design work from concept to delivery. Responsible for recruiting and organizing the design team, presenting to stakeholders, and ensuring seamless collaboration across all disciplines under extreme time pressure.

## Process

### Pre-Discovery & Guidelines Analysis

Conducted rapid pre-discovery phase analyzing existing risk management solutions and studying Google's Material Design guidelines and internal UI standards. This accelerated the design process by establishing a solid foundation from day one.

![Requirements analysis and feature mapping](/images/cases/osra/requirements.png)

### User Meetings & Requirements Clarification

Organized intensive user meetings with Google representatives to rapidly clarify requirements that were initially undefined. Conducted focused interviews to understand risk management workflows, user roles, and critical pain points across Alphabet's diverse enterprise landscape.

### Information Architecture

Defined role-based sitemaps for three distinct user types, ensuring each persona had an optimized navigation path through the risk management workflow.

### Testing & Validation

Conducted multiple testing rounds with Google representatives to validate the prototype against real-world risk management scenarios. Each round informed refinements that progressively aligned the solution with user expectations and business requirements.

## Results

Transformed a complex UX tool request into a streamlined scenario logic that was easy to understand and navigate. Proved that both desktop and mobile solutions were viable within the proposed architecture. The prototype aligned with user expectations and was approved by stakeholders, laying the foundation for the next product development phase.

## Key Learnings

Gained invaluable experience working with a large vendor under extremely tight time constraints. Learned the importance of identifying potential issues early in the process and establishing clear communication channels from day one. The project reinforced that rapid delivery does not have to compromise design quality when processes are well-organized.
