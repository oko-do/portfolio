## About the Project

Ketcher is a popular open-source web editor for drawing complex chemical and molecular structures. The tool was built for chemists and researchers worldwide. I joined the project on my own initiative (beyond my regular responsibilities) to take its UX/UI to the next level.

## My Role

As Lead Designer, I took on the challenge of building design processes from scratch and preparing the third version (v3) for release. My goals were to introduce UX research practices, develop a scalable design system, and create a knowledge base for fast onboarding of new contributors.

## Process

### UX Research & Analytics

For the first time, I integrated a systematic approach to research into the project. I developed user personas, customer journey maps (CJMs), and conducted usability audits to understand how real chemists interact with editors.

![User flow: mapping key interactions in the chemical editor](/images/cases/ketcher/user-flow.png)

### Complex Interface Redesign

I completely reworked the workspace — introducing support for 2D and 3D molecular visualization, convenient compound search, a template library, and an updated interactive periodic table.

### Scalable Design System

I designed a design system from scratch, including a custom icon set for specialized chemical tools. The system's architecture proved so successful that it later became the foundation for a related product — Peptide Editor.

![Custom icon set for the editor tools](/images/cases/ketcher/custom-icons.png)

![Peptide Module: a module that evolved into a new product, built on the same design system](/images/cases/ketcher/default-layout.png)

### DesignOps & Process Management

I aligned the design roadmap with the development timeline. I organized a transparent file structure and assembled a unified knowledge base (guidelines and decision logs), which radically simplified designer onboarding into the open-source community.

![Project timeline: design roadmap aligned with development milestones](/images/cases/ketcher/timeline.png)

## Impact

- Successful Ketcher v3 release with a modern, intuitive interface and a continuous improvement cycle based on community feedback
- The design system proved its scalability and was reused for a new product (Peptide Editor)
- Completely eliminated chaos in files and processes: established structured collaboration between the design team and independent developers

## Key Learnings

The project gave me great experience in quickly immersing myself in a new, scientific domain. The main challenge was successfully adapting corporate design processes to the realities of a decentralized open-source team.
