## About the Project

Alcon is one of the world's largest manufacturers of medical devices and vision care products. The Custom Pak Configurator is an internal B2B application used by sales teams globally to configure surgical packs and manage orders across multiple regions and product lines.

## The Challenge

Create and support a new version of the internal B2B application to optimize global sales processes. Continue the redesign combined with incremental bug fixing and competitive analysis.

![Legacy interface of the Custom Pak application](/images/cases/alcon/legacy-interface.png)

## My Role

Senior Designer covering the full product lifecycle — from research and discovery through design, prototyping, testing, and iterative improvement.

## Process

### SCRUM Framework & Research Discovery

Worked within a SCRUM framework with two-week sprints. Conducted research and discovery phases including stakeholder interviews, sales team observation sessions, and analysis of existing workflows to understand pain points in the surgical pack configuration process.

### Design & Prototyping

Together with stakeholders and the development team, we ran design sessions to find optimal solutions. As a result, we developed several concept directions, filtering them through technical feasibility, business value, and UX criteria.

High-fidelity interactive prototypes were designed for testing with real users.

A convenient component library for web and mobile was also created, enabling a consistent UI and well-defined user interaction patterns.

### User Testing & Feedback

Conducted multiple rounds of user testing including A/B testing and Treejack testing for information architecture validation. Gathered quantitative and qualitative feedback from sales representatives across different regions to validate design decisions.

![UX tree testing results for information architecture](/images/cases/alcon/ux-tree-testing.png)

## Results

Delivered an optimized cross-platform application that significantly improved key UX metrics: Task Success Rate, Time-on-Task, and System Usability Scale (SUS) scores all showed measurable improvement over the previous version.
