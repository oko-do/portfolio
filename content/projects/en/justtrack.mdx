## About the Project

Justtrack is a comprehensive mobile measurement and marketing automation platform (MMP+) that empowers app and game publishers to scale globally by unifying advanced attribution, real-time monetization analytics, and automated campaign optimization into a single, high-performance dashboard.

## The Challenge

My main tasks were to bring order to the interface: I designed and implemented a scalable design system. The goal was to make the platform more intuitive and reduce cognitive load on users, as well as develop a number of new features from scratch (for example, launching the AI-powered Smart Insights module with new analytics interaction patterns). Additionally, I conducted a usability audit of problem areas, optimising filtering and data perception where users previously struggled.

## Process

### Design System Audit & Foundation

We conducted a comprehensive audit of UI components across the platform, identifying critical inconsistencies. Based on the audit, a detailed documentation and categorisation system was created, which became the foundation for future roadmap planning. We aimed to group related UX/UI issues into unified clusters. All recorded findings were categorised accordingly, and design tickets were created based on this analysis.

![UX audit: interface problem areas overview](/images/cases/justtrack/audit-ux-review.png)

### Building the Design System

Working on the system wasn't just "drawing components" — it was about building a bridge between design and engineering. The process looked like this:

1. **Reverse engineering & inventory.** We started with "excavations" — conducted a full platform audit, catalogued every unique element, and built a component registry. To avoid getting lost in the details, we used Atomic Design methodology, breaking everything down into Atoms, Molecules, and Organisms.

![Design system components registry](/images/cases/justtrack/design-system-components-list.png)

2. **Prioritisation & strategy.** We didn't try to do everything at once. We prioritised and started from the base — atoms (colours, fonts, buttons). For each element we ran a series of refinements, consulting closely with developers to ensure our Figma decisions mapped naturally to their stack (a mix of React MUI and custom solutions).

![GitLab ticket selection for design system tasks](/images/cases/justtrack/select-ticket-gitlab.png)

3. **Shared workspace (Kanban).** To keep the process transparent, we set up a shared Kanban board in GitLab. Product Owners were involved — during review stages they helped align our ideas with business goals and shared improvement requests.

4. **Code sync & competitive analysis.** We studied best practices on the market, but our engineers were the primary reference point. We met development halfway, adapting the Figma structure to match code logic and minimise friction during handoff.

![Selects UI matrix from Figma](/images/cases/justtrack/selects-ui-matrix-from-figma.png)

5. **Quality control & Source of Truth.** After each implementation I conducted a Design Review, verifying that production matched the intent. After implementing several Atomic components, we decided to create a Storybook — now our primary source of truth.

### Navigation Redesign: Shift to an App-Centric Model

**Problem:** The previous navigation was overloaded both visually and functionally, creating excessive cognitive load. The use of dozens of filters was tailored to a narrow group of top managers, while interaction paths for regular users remained inconsistent and confusing.

<ImageGrid
  src1="/images/cases/justtrack/old-navi.png"
  alt1="Old navigation structure with loaded funnel progression view"
  src2="/images/cases/justtrack/old-navi-funnel.png"
  alt2="Old navigation: funnel progression detail"
/>

**Research & insights:** We analysed user workflows, studied competitors, and conducted surveys with our audience. It turned out that most users don't need global segmentation — they work with 1–2 specific apps. Based on this data, we refined user personas and focused on their real use cases.

**Solution:** An app-centric model was proposed and validated, allowing users to switch between tasks seamlessly.

![New navigation: final implementation](/images/cases/justtrack/new-navigation.png)

Key changes:

**Contextual management:** We moved away from a complex filter system in favour of working within the context of the selected app. This made the interface visually cleaner and statistics easier to digest.

**Two-level hierarchy:** To clearly separate access levels, we introduced two-layer navigation.

**Colour coding:** To help users instantly understand their current context, levels were visually differentiated: blue was assigned to individual app interfaces, and yellow to the corporate level (business units).

### Smart Insights: Automating Root-Cause Analysis

The main challenge was to design an interface for a new module that transforms dozens of scattered notifications into a concise feed of prioritised insights. This eliminated visual noise and the need to manually hunt for anomalies across general dashboards. The solution is built on ML-driven analytics that highlights gaps between forecast and actuals.

The centrepiece of the design is an interactive card with a 14-day plan-vs-actual comparison chart. For deep-dive investigation (drill-down), an adaptive table with a three-level hierarchy is used (e.g. App → Partner → Country).

![A typical Smart Insight example: multiple apps affected](/images/cases/justtrack/diagnostics-table.png)

## Key Results

- Implemented a scalable design system that reduced time-to-market for new features and ensured visual consistency across the entire ecosystem.

- Completely reimagined the platform's navigation model, transitioning to a modern app-centric UX.

- Designed the Smart Insights module from scratch, transforming raw data into actionable insights for users.

- Improved information perception quality through a redesign of filtering systems, making data interaction intuitive.

- AI-Driven Prototyping. Introduced a process for rapidly building and testing functional prototypes using Google AI Studio, enabling validation of complex product hypotheses early on without frontend resources.

- Integrated Claude into the design system management workflow to automate documentation writing, design token generation, and component consistency auditing.
