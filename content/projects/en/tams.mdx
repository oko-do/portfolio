## About the Project

ADNOC (Abu Dhabi National Oil Company) is the state-owned oil company of the United Arab Emirates, engaged in exploration, production, and refining of oil and gas. TAMS (Turnaround Management System) is a smart enterprise application for administration and optimization of oil manufacturing processes.

## The Challenge

Develop the design for a smart TAMS application in collaboration with IBM engineers and Accenture consultants. The system covers administration and optimization of oil manufacturing processes, risk accounting, and workforce planning.

## My Role

Lead Designer overseeing all design aspects of the TAMS application, from information architecture to visual design. Coordinated with multiple vendor teams and ensured design consistency across all modules.

## Results

Delivered a system that enabled faster task execution and improved overall productivity for ADNOC's turnaround operations. The intuitive design reduced the learning curve for new operators. Systematic risk management capabilities were integrated with existing SCADA/ERP systems. Created a reusable UI asset library that accelerated development of subsequent modules.

## Key Learnings

Gained deep experience working with enterprise-scale oil & gas operations, understanding the critical importance of safety and reliability in industrial UI design. Learned to coordinate effectively across multiple vendor teams (IBM, Accenture) with different working methodologies and cultural backgrounds.
