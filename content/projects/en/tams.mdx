## About the Project

ADNOC is the National Oil Company of the United Arab Emirates. TAMS (Turnaround Management System) is a smart enterprise application for managing oil refinery turnarounds, risk assessment, and resource planning.

## My Role

As Lead Designer, I oversaw the full design cycle — from information architecture to visual design — in close partnership with IBM engineers and Accenture consultants. The main goal was to create an intuitive interface for a complex industrial system.

## Process

### Analysis & Discovery

The work began with a deep dive into oil & gas industry standards. Together with BAs (business analysts), I decomposed ADNOC's massive technical specifications into clear user scenarios, identifying the bottleneck in existing planning processes.

![Mindmap](/images/cases/tams/mindmap.png)

### Prototyping & Iterations

Given the system's complexity, I used a "data-to-interface" approach. We created low-fidelity (Lo-Fi) prototypes to validate refinery logic and risk management flows with IBM engineers, then iterated them into high-fidelity mockups, verifying compliance with guidelines and ERP system constraints.

### AI Integration — Cold Eye

I designed an environment for working with AI-powered predictive reports. The system automatically verifies facility readiness status, identifies potential schedule risks, and suggests corrective actions — ensuring transparency and safety during the planning phase.

![Cold Eye report: AI-driven independent assessment of turnaround readiness](/images/cases/tams/cold-eye-report.png)

### Request Management

I optimized the work and resource request creation flow, reducing complex tables to clear, unified forms.

![Create request: unified form for turnaround activity initiation](/images/cases/tams/create-request.png)

## Impact

- Increased overall speed and efficiency of task execution by ADNOC operators
- Reduced onboarding time: intuitive design significantly shortened the learning curve for new employees
- Risk management interface seamlessly integrated with existing enterprise systems (SCADA and ERP)
- Built a scalable UI library (Design System) that significantly accelerated development and launch of subsequent modules

## Key Learnings

Working on TAMS gave me a deep understanding of the demanding enterprise segment in the oil & gas industry, where safety and reliability come first. I also successfully established design processes within cross-functional collaboration with complex stakeholders (IBM and Accenture teams), uniting different methodologies and work cultures.
